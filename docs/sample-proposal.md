# Website Redesign Proposal

**Client:** Acme Corp
**Agency:** Digital Innovators
**Effective Date:** April 14, 2026

## 1. Project Overview

This proposal outlines the scope of work for the complete redesign of the Acme Corp corporate website. The goal is to improve conversion rates, modernize the brand identity, and ensure full mobile responsiveness.

## 2. In-Scope Deliverables

The Agency will provide the following deliverables:

- **Custom Pages:** Design and development of exactly 5 custom page templates (Home, About Us, Services, Case Studies, Contact).
- **CMS Integration:** Integration of a headless CMS for content management of the 5 pages mentioned above.
- **SEO Optimization:** Basic on-page SEO setup (Meta tags, title tags, alt text) for the 5 custom pages.
- **Revisions:** Up to two (2) rounds of revisions during the design phase.

## 3. Out-of-Scope Items

The following items are explicitly out of scope for this initial engagement. If requested, they will require a separate addendum and additional billing:

- **E-commerce Functionality:** Shopping carts, payment gateways, or product catalogues are not included.
- **Custom Illustrations or Video Production:** All imagery must be stock photos or provided by the client.
- **Ongoing Maintenance:** Content updates or software patches after the launch date.
- **Copywriting:** All text content must be provided by the client.
- **Third-Party Integrations:** CRM integrations (e.g., Salesforce, HubSpot) or complex API integrations are excluded.

## 4. Communication & Support

- **Support Hours:** Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST.
- **Response Time:** We guarantee a 24-hour response time during normal support hours. Weekends and holidays are excluded.

## 5. Timeline

The expected project duration is 6 weeks from the kickoff date, assuming timely client feedback.
